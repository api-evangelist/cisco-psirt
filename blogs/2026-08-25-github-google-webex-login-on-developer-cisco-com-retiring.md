Quick heads up for anyone with a developer.cisco.com account: GitHub, Google, and Webex login are retiring on August 31, 2026. Going forward you'll need to sign in with Cisco SSO (this includes access to the DevNet sandboxes). If you use the same email address, your profile and history stay intact, so nothing gets lost in the switch.
